A. Single-common-predecessor subclass: algebra APPROVED, draft pending final read.
Assume the S102 common-downset and neutral-chain setup and D={d}.
Since n cannot precede d and N is a chain, there is a k, 0<=k<=R,
such that d is incomparable to n1,...,nk and d<n_(k+1),...,n_R.
Then a_j=min(j,k)+1, c_j=1 for j<=k and 0 afterwards.
Consequently f_j=(j+1)(j+2)/2 for j<=k, and f_j>0 afterwards.
Let m_j=E_j>0 and S=sum_(j=0)^k m_j. The actual direction obeys
sum_(j=0)^k m_j g_j=0. The scalar inequality being proved for k<=3
is stronger: it holds for ANY such scalar g, with no backend direction restriction.
Deleting the next neutral minimal point injects LE(Q_(j+1)) into LE(Q_j),
so m_j>=m_(j+1) (indeed the u,y alternatives give strictness).
The variance identity is
 S sum m_j g_j^2 = sum_(a<b) m_a m_b (g_a-g_b)^2.
Retain only energy terms j<k; all discarded terms are nonnegative.
Set delta_i=g_i-g_(i+1). The required difference times S is delta^T B delta,
where
 B = S diag(F_i m_i) - sum_(a<b) m_a m_b v_ab v_ab^T,
 F_i=(i+1)(i+2)/2, and (v_ab)_i=1 if a<=i<b and 0 otherwise.
Independent symbolic expansion, with generic indeterminates m, gives:
 k=1: leading minor m0^2.
 k=2: leading minors m0^2; m0^2 S(3m1-m2).
 k=3: leading minors m0^2; m0^2 S(3m1-m2-m3);
       m0^2 S^2(18m1m2-3m1m3-6m2^2-5m2m3).
These are symbolic identities, not numerical sample checks. For k=3 the last
bracket is at least 12m2^2-8m2m3 >=4m2^2>0, since the coefficient of m1
is 18m2-3m3>0 and m1>=m2>=m3>0. The second bracket is >=m1>0.
The k=2 and k=1 cases are immediate. Sylvester's criterion proves positivity.
For k=0 the weighted mean condition forces g0=0; retained variance is zero.
Combining with S102 energy-minus-variance inequality establishes H^2>=Ru Ry
for this subclass, including R=k and R=0. It does NOT prove the unrestricted
single-predecessor case: k is the number of neutral points incomparable to d,
not the cardinality of D or the total neutral chain length R.

B. Full backend Schur supplement: independently derived, author draft pending.
For j<R write a=E_j>0, b=E_(j+1)>0, c=E_(j+2)>=0,
Delta=b^2-ac>=0, x=h^T C_j, y=h^T C_(j+1).
The genuine backend first-two-step matrix has at most one positive eigenvalue.
Compressing to its all-minima and neutral coordinate gives K=[[a,b],[b,c]].
If Delta>0, K has inertia (+,-), so its Schur complement has no positive
eigenvalue. Hence
 -h^T V_j h >= (c x^2-2bxy+a y^2)/Delta.
Substitution x=b g_(j+1)-a g_j, y=c g_(j+2)-b g_(j+1)
shows this is precisely S102 local bound (D) plus
 (c/Delta)[a(g_j-g_(j+1))+b(g_(j+2)-g_(j+1))]^2.
This formula includes j=R-1: c=0, Delta=b^2>0, supplement=0.
No division by c is needed in the final formula.

Full exact release-matrix decomposition reported by the author is also
independently derivable. Write z=C_(j+1)-(b/a)C_j, J=[[1,-1],[-1,1]].
After projecting out the positive all-minima direction a, the remaining
3x3 Schur matrix is negative semidefinite. For Delta>0, its scalar entry
is -Delta/a. Projecting it out gives
 V_j=C_j C_j^T/a-(a/Delta)zz^T-kappa_j J, kappa_j>=0.
To justify the J form rather than assume it: the identities
 V_j 1=C_j-C_(j+1), 1^T C_j=a-b, and 1^T z=Delta/a
show that the PSD residual annihilates 1. A symmetric 2x2 PSD matrix
annihilating 1 is kappa J, with kappa>=0.
If B_j is the actual 3x3 first-two-step matrix, determinant expansion gives
 det B_j=kappa_j Delta. This identity uses the determinant-one change of
basis (e_u,e_y,all-minima), so no scale factor is missing.

For Delta=0, the same negative semidefinite Schur matrix has zero scalar
diagonal. PSD zero-diagonal row/column compatibility forces z=0. Thus
 V_j=C_j C_j^T/a-kappa_j J, kappa_j>=0.
The relation z=0 entails
 a(g_j-g_(j+1))+b(g_(j+2)-g_(j+1))=0.
Under this compatibility S102's baseline equals -x^2/a exactly, so the
curvature term is defined as zero at Delta=0, and the only extra term is
kappa_j(h_u-h_y)^2. One must NOT substitute 0/0 or claim an arbitrary limit.
At j=R, the separate exact identity uses kappa_R=-det(V_R)/E_R>=0,
and -h^T V_R h=-E_R g_R^2+kappa_R(h_u-h_y)^2.

The resulting local/global exact identities retain actual backend information.
They do not by themselves imply a nonnegative global sum: energy minus variance
can still have a negative contribution. For probability direction h=(1-p,-p),
h_u-h_y=1. Any eventual universal nonnegativity claim needs a proof using
realizable C_j,V_j from one poset, not merely arbitrary mean-zero scalar g.
