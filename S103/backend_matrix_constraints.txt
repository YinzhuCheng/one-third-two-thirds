1. EXACT SCHUR DECOMPOSITION, INCLUDING DEGENERACY
For one index write a=E_j,b=E_{j+1},c=E_{j+2}, C=C_j, C'=C_{j+1}, V=V_j, and Delta=b^2-ac. Let
 z=C'-(b/a)C,
 J=[[1,-1],[-1,1]].
Since T has at most one positive eigenvalue and a>0, Delta>=0. Eliminating the positive a coordinate from M gives the negative-semidefinite matrix
 [[V-CC^T/a,z],[z^T,-Delta/a]].
Equivalently K=CC^T/a-V is positive semidefinite, and
 [[K,-z],[-z^T,Delta/a]] >= 0.

If Delta>0, then K-(a/Delta)zz^T is positive semidefinite. The row-sum identities give
 K1=z,
 z^T1=Delta/a.
Therefore this residual annihilates 1, and since it is 2x2 it is exactly kappa J with kappa>=0. Consequently
 V=CC^T/a-(a/Delta)zz^T-kappa J.                    (1)
Moreover
 kappa=det(B_j)/Delta>=0.                          (2)
To check (2), the change of basis from B_j to the three columns e_u,1_all,e_neutral has determinant +/-1. Its scalar 2x2 block T has determinant -Delta and its scalar Schur residual is -kappa.

If Delta=0, positive semidefiniteness forces z=0: a C'=b C. Then K1=0 and
 V=CC^T/a-kappa J,
 kappa=C_u^2/a-V_uu>=0.                           (3)
This avoids every zero denominator. For the terminal j=R, b=c=0 and (3) applies, with
 kappa_R=-det(V_R)/E_R.
For j=R-1, c=0,b=E_R>0 so Delta=b^2>0, and (1)-(2) apply normally.

2. ADDITIONAL CHECKABLE CONSTRAINTS ON A TRUE DIRECTION
Let h be any fixed real two-vector, x_j=h^T C_j, and g_j=-sum_{l>=j}x_l/E_j, with g_{R+1}=g_{R+2}=0. Thus x_j=b g_{j+1}-a g_j.
For Delta>0, the exact projected identity is
 -h^T V_j h = (c x_j^2-2b x_jx_{j+1}+a x_{j+1}^2)/Delta
               +kappa_j(h_u-h_y)^2.               (4)
For Delta=0 the real direction MUST satisfy a x_{j+1}=b x_j (not just weighted mean zero). The vector identity a C'=b C is stronger and directly checkable from counts.

The nonnegative diagonal release counts yield, for each entry coordinate k and Delta>0,
 (a/Delta)(C'_k-(b/a)C_k)^2+kappa_j <= C_k^2/a.    (5)
Nonnegative off-diagonal counts likewise constrain the signed cross term in (1). The full matrix (1), nonnegative entry counts, and exact recurrence are therefore much stronger than scalar log-concavity alone.

A further elementary probabilistic constraint, independent of CP, is
 0<=E_j q_j-E_{j+1}q_{j+1}<=E_j-E_{j+1},          (6)
because the middle expression is C_{j,u}. Thus q_j=(1-r_j)theta_j+r_j q_{j+1}, where r_j=E_{j+1}/E_j and theta_j=C_{j,u}/(E_j-E_{j+1}) belongs to [0,1]. This alone does not imply monotonicity of q.

3. EXACT UPGRADE OF S102 ENERGY MINUS VARIANCE
Set A_j=E_j g_j^2. For j<R define
 P_j=2A_{j+1}-A_j-A_{j+2}+E_j(g_j-g_{j+1})^2,
and P_R=-A_R.
For j<R with Delta_j>0 let
 t_j=E_j(g_{j+1}-g_j)-E_{j+1}(g_{j+2}-g_{j+1}),
 L_j=E_{j+2} t_j^2/Delta_j.
For Delta_j=0 put L_j=0; compatibility forces t_j=0 when E_{j+2}>0. Put L_R=0. In particular L_{R-1}=0, since E_{R+1}=0.
Direct expansion of (4), or (3) in the degenerate case, gives the EXACT identity
 -h^T V_j h=P_j+L_j+kappa_j(h_u-h_y)^2.           (7)
The degenerate case is not defined by taking an unproved limit. If j<R and Delta=0, b,c>0, compatibility yields t=0; substitution gives -hVh=P_j+kappa(h_u-h_y)^2. The separate R endpoint follows from (3).

Multiplying by f_j and summing yields the exact refinement
 -h^T W h = sum_{j<R} f_j E_j(g_j-g_{j+1})^2
             -sum_{j<=R} c_j E_j g_j^2
             +sum_{j<R} f_j L_j
             +(h_u-h_y)^2 sum_{j<=R} f_j kappa_j. (8)
The last two terms are separately nonnegative. This preserves precisely a curvature penalty and a rank-one negative residual that were discarded in S102. It is an identity, not a proof that the entire right side is nonnegative.

For h=(1-p,-p), g_j=p-q_j and h_u-h_y=1, so
 E(p^2-U)=energy-variance+curvature+backend_slack,
 where curvature=sum f_jL_j,
       backend_slack=sum f_jkappa_j>=0.             (9)
Dropping only backend_slack gives a strictly stronger sufficient scalar inequality than S102. Dropping both recovers S102 exactly.

5. POSSIBLE UNIVERSAL SECOND-ORDER SCALAR INEQUALITY, NOT PROVED
Numerical spectral experiments suggest the strengthened inequality
 sum c_jE_jg_j^2 <= sum f_jE_j(g_j-g_{j+1})^2+sum f_jL_j
 under sum c_jE_jg_j=0
 may hold for arbitrary g if a is nondecreasing log-concave and E is decreasing log-concave, with the stated degenerate compatibility. This is a conjectural next lemma, not an established result. A test of 10000 randomly generated strictly monotone LC a/E sequences of length 3 to 16 found no negative eigenvalue. Numerical positive results do not exclude other sequences or numerical errors.
For a_j=binom(d+j,j), E_j=(R-j+2)(R-j+1), the minimum eigenvalue of (energy+curvature-variance)/variance on mean-zero g numerically equals 3/d for R in {2,4,8,16,32}, d in {1,2,4,8}. This surprising simple value is a possible solvable special family. No analytic proof is supplied here.
The experiments are in probe_schur_spectral.py. The actual real-direction search is independently testing the strengthened lower bound. Until a valid proof or counterexample emerges, the determinant target stays OPEN.
