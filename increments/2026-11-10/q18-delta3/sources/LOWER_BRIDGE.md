## 3. What transfers when Dc has a unique physical minimum

Suppose the ORIGINAL Dc has a fixed minimum x below its other two
points (chain or V shape). Partition all actual original extension words
by their literal relative order tau on Dc. Let P_tau add that Dc order.
Every such order extends the actual Dc order, and the sets LE(P_tau)
are disjoint with union LE(P). This is a true integer-word partition,
not a uniform averaging of completions or a majority assertion on them.

Each completion retains original seed signatures: adding Dc comparisons
does not add a forbidden pivot predecessor, and their Y/O successors
were already shared. The actual O and Q size, population deficits and
occupied classes are unchanged. Incomparability degrees can only fall.
No new a-only child appears (all Dc already have b,c predecessors), so
the unique original u/v fork is unchanged. Every other original port
is retained, possibly with transitive consequences. The same physical
x remains the bottom of EVERY completed Dc chain and D(x)={a,b,c}.

The accepted chain raw bank is unconditional, so

 Bx(P)=sum_tau Bx(P_tau)>0.

This equation is valid because BOTH defining events Kba and K(c<v<x)
are fixed events of the SAME labels throughout the disjoint word fibers.
It would NOT hold for a different word-selected bottom in each fiber.
Likewise, wherever a chain cap is available,

 2N(P)-3K_P(x<v)=sum_tau(2N(P_tau)-3K_Ptau(x<v))>0.

The latter presently applies at k4. No completion is assumed NB.
Applying section2 then closes all original k4 sources with a unique
minimum of Dc, without any original Cv/A chain or x-hole assumption.
No fixed-minimum bank is asserted for Dc with two/three minima.
