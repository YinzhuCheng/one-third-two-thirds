# OC2: a finite exception frontier and stronger asymmetric-side blockers

2026-11-10. **Analytic author candidate, independent review requested.** Structural necessary conditions, not a new probability payment for the remaining case.

## Contract

Retain OC1's zero-x-frontier m6k7 exact lift: Mx=E disjoint-union {f}, where |E|=6, f is an empty-signature C-minimum, and there is exactly one full-signature g incomparable with f. Write

    D=D_C(g), a=|D|, W=C−(E union {f,g}).

OC1 establishes that D is a nonempty E ideal, 1≤a≤6, and every W label lies above both x and f. These are original object facts; W is not an autonomous or uniformly weighted replacement.

## 1. Finite noncommon frontier

No W label is below g, since the full C predecessor set of g is D⊆E. Define

    J={w in W:w incomparable with g}, T=W−J.

Then T consists exactly of the W labels above g. The full-signature g has no outside-C incomparable labels. Its complete C incomparability list is f, E−D, and J. Therefore

    deg_(P+)(g)=1+(6−a)+|J|,
    |J|≤a+3≤9.

The ideal property of D ensures that an E point not in D cannot be above g: g<E would contradict E being a C ideal. Thus the degree equality does not omit an alternative E relation.

J is an ideal in W. A predecessor in W of a J label cannot lie above g, or that J label would also lie above g. It follows that

    K=E union {f,g} union J

is a genuine C ideal, of size at most17. The remaining T is above both f and g, and every T label has full signature. Relations of T to E−D and J remain arbitrary subject to the original order and degrees; neither uniform tail weights nor ordinal-cap factorization is asserted.

## 2. A new common blocker for the root asymmetric sides

Define the original root asymmetric sides

    A=U_C(y)−U_C(z), B=U_C(z)−U_C(y).

E and g contain both roots; f contains neither; T is full. Therefore A,B⊆J. In particular all their labels are above x and f, and each is incomparable with g.

For p∈A, the following original incomparable neighbors are disjoint: all six E labels, g, root z, all B labels, and all A-internal incomparable neighbors. Hence

    deg_A(p)≤2−|B|.

The symmetric statement holds for B. These are induced degree restrictions only, not permission to lift an internal guard through unspecified external relations.

If A is nonchain and B is nonempty, choose incomparable p,q∈A. They have at least nine common incomparable neighbors other than each other: E (six), g, z, and a point of B. Actual Delta10 forces their entire remaining neighborhoods to be these blockers. Every point outside the two endpoints and these specified common incomparable blockers is comparable with both p,q and cannot lie between them; the blockers themselves remain incomparable with both; therefore p,q have identical full strict lower and upper sets. This is the accepted original exact-twin guard. Additional B labels only make the degree premise infeasible. Thus outside original twins, a nonchain A forces B empty, and conversely.

If both sides are chains, the original roots y,z satisfy the OLD VG configuration. Therefore outside both original twins and root VG, **exactly one side is nonchain and the other is empty**.

On that nonchain side, say A, internal incomparability degree is at most2. A three-antichain would consume, at each endpoint, the common eight blockers E,g,z plus the other two endpoints. Its members would therefore be original full twins by the same saturation argument. Thus outside twins,

    width(A)≤2.

Finally the original z has four outside-C incomparable labels b,x,y,u and is also incomparable with f and every A label. Hence |A|≤5. This size bound does not make A autonomous.

## 3. What an unguarded candidate would still need

LG1 guarantees an internal positive VG configuration in the nonchain side of internal degree≤2. It is not automatically an original VG: other labels of J or the full tail may distinguish its endpoints. E labels are uniform incomparable blockers and cannot be active distinguishing ports. NP1's port analysis, with g added as an actual uniform incomparable blocker, must be applied rather than treating the signature side as a module.

Thus a candidate outside all explicit guards must simultaneously exhibit:

- a nonempty E ideal D below g and a true J ideal with |J|≤|D|+3;
- exactly one nonchain root-asymmetric side inside J, of size≤5, width≤2 and internal degree≤2;
- the other root-asymmetric side empty;
- actual distinguishing ports defeating every internal positive and dual VG candidate, with the original endpoint degree budgets;
- the complete E/J/T relations and their true linear-extension multiplicities if a probability argument is attempted.

These are necessary constraints, not a realizability certificate. The full tail can have many uniform successors and is not proved removable. The two private differences between x and f remain {g} on the upper side and {b} on the lower side; nothing here treats their joint size as one or invokes a product≤2 shortcut.

The only payment deductions used above return configurations to existing exact-twin or VG guards. This manuscript does not solve the remaining m6k7 case, assert fixed-(u,b) balance there, or construct an object escaping all guards.
