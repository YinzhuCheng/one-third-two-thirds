# RC1: a two-cover signature ideal closes without OLD moment

Date: 2026-11-10 UTC. Analytic author candidate, uninvolved review requested. Exact original B1 private-predecessor lift; actual full Delta10; 0<=m<=k<=7. All events, degrees and word counts belong to the original full lift.

## Theorem
Assume every label of Mx=C-U(x) has complete old T-signature yz. Equivalently every C label is above x or is above both y,z (and misses x). Then at least one of the physical original pairs (u,b),(y,b),(z,b) is balanced, without OLD seed moment. For k>=5 the physical bank has strict lower payment S>N; the smaller k follow from the reviewed scalar Phi and DG1 gates.

The condition allows C-minima of signature{x}, so it does not imply TB2's support>=2 premise. No fixed-u/b guarantee is asserted for the whole class. This is not full B1 or a net-new ordinary-population claim.

## Original ideal and degree caps
M=Mx is a C ideal: if t<s in C with s missing x, then t cannot lie above x. Under the signature hypothesis each M label lies above y,z, a,b and misses x; it is above u unless it belongs to E. Thus each M label has at least the outside-C incomparable neighbor x, giving its original C-incomparability degree<=9. E labels satisfy the stronger cap8, unused here. This is a full original point-degree argument, not an inherited bound on a deleted object.

Every C label contains x or contains yz, so A0=0. Fix a true beta in LE(C), and for k>=1 let H be its number of letters before the first M label. If beta starts in M then hy=hz0; if outside M then hx0. As in DG1, the literal initial-prefix-one kernel is

    a1(beta)=hy hz+min(hy,hz)<=H(H+1).

It counts the gaps for y,z after the one initial root x, including the extra ordering at an equal gap. There are no other K1 initial-root types.

## Whole-ideal insertion with true fiber weights
Fix an actual tau in LE(M) and gamma in LE(C-M). No M label has a retained predecessor, by ideality. Its deadline is the number of gamma labels before its first gamma successor, each incomparable with it; hence every deadline is<=9.

The entire ideal M need not be an antichain. Its internal comparisons are respected by the fixed tau. Legal interleavings are exactly the increasing selected-position tuples whose coordinate gaps are bounded by these actual deadlines. Formal end padding to9 retained positions, if necessary, is count-preserving because all actual deadlines remain bounded by the original retained length. The feasible set is a nonempty down-set in the uniform distributive lattice of k-subsets of9+k slots. FKG therefore bounds the increasing statistic H(H+1) by the unrestricted first-selected gap H0 with9 ordered non-M tokens and k ordered M tokens.

The exact first-hit factorial moments give

    EH0=9/(k+1),
    E[H0(H0-1)]=144/((k+1)(k+2)),
    E[H0(H0+1)]=18(k+10)/((k+1)(k+2)).

Every actual tau/gamma fiber has that same upper bound on its mean a1. Multiply by EACH actual fiber cardinality and sum, not by equal weights on different words:

    A1/e(C)<=18(k+10)/((k+1)(k+2)).

For k>=5 the bound decreases with k and is at most45/7<7. The original-count bank gate gives

    S-N>=14e(C)-2(A1+A0)>=(8/7)e(C)>0.

The original candidate-wise upper caps are u/b<=5/9, y/b,z/b<=1/2, so the bank yields a strict balanced original pair.

For k<=3 all feasible m<=k are in the pure scalar Phi safe region. At k4, m<=3 are also in that region (m3 gives the closed Phi0 endpoint), while m4 is DG1's diagonal. The case k0 is the separate unconditional F>=48. These cover all remaining feasible cells. No OLD moment is used at any stage.

## Consequence for the real remaining contract
Suppose an actual object has only one x-containing C-minimum and has no support<=1 C-minimum missing x. Every C-minimum inside M then has signature yz: a missing-x signature of support at least2 can only be yz. Every M label lies above an M-minimum, so its signature also contains yz and misses x; hence all M signatures are exactly yz. RC1 therefore pays this whole branch.

In particular a still-unpaid zero/one-frontier object MUST have a C-minimum missing x with support<=1. This conclusion also holds with zero x-frontier because any low-support minimum already misses x. It eliminates the putative branch whose sole low-support minimum was the signature{x} frontier point; it does not assert that the surviving constraints escape all older guards or are jointly realizable.
