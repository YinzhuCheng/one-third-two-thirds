## 2. A root degree cap that the loose fiber domain omitted

## 4. Delete f, retaining the actual gamma fibers

Delete only the actual C-minimum f and fix any gamma in LE(C-f). Its legal f-insertion gaps are exactly 0..d, ending just before its first f-successor. Because f is incomparable with exactly E union {g}, d<=7. Let

    h=h_x(gamma)=q(gamma), 0<=h<=6.

The equality holds because the only missing-x labels in C-f are the E labels. All h initial E labels are incomparable with f, so d>=h.

### 4a. h=0: the first gamma label is an actual f-child

The first gamma label contains x. It cannot be g, since g has a nonempty E predecessor ideal. Therefore it lies in W and is above f. Thus d=0, and beta=f gamma is the only fiber word. Its runs are H=1 and, by (2), 1<=Y<=4, 1<=Z<=6. Formula (3) and the exact kernels give

    F_beta=56+10(Y+Z)+2YZ+2min(Y,Z),
    G_beta=26-5(Y+Z)-4YZ-4min(Y,Z),
    2F_beta+3G_beta
       =190+5(Y+Z)-8YZ-8min(Y,Z) >= 16.          (4)

The last expression decreases in either variable throughout Y,Z>=1: on either side of its single kink the slope in Y is either 5-8Z or 5-8Z-8, both negative, and symmetrically for Z. Its minimum on the allowed rectangle is therefore at (4,6), where it equals 16.

### 4b. 1<=h<=6: gamma starts in E

At f-gap 0, beta begins with f followed immediately by an E label, so (H,Y,Z)=(h+1,1,1). Equations (3) and the exact kernels give

    F_0=80+8h, G_0=8-2h,
    2F_0+3G_0=184+10h.                         (5)

At every gap j>=1 beta begins in E. Therefore Y=Z=0 and all original A0/A1 prefix terms vanish. For 1<=j<=h,

    H=h+1, Q=j,
    2F_j+3G_j=180+(30-22j)(h+1)+11j(j-1).     (6)

For every later legal gap j>h, the initial E run is h and the initial missing-x run is h, so

    2F_j+3G_j=180+19h-11h^2.                   (7)

This also covers E labels that occur after g: their individual positions can extend d but do not restart either initial run. No erroneous d<=h+1 assumption is made.

Summing (5)-(6) over the mandatory gaps 0..h gives

    B(h)=184+(616h+24h^2-22h^3)/3.

For integer 1<=h<=5, the extra-gap coefficient (7) is nonnegative: it is a concave quadratic with endpoint values 188 and 0. Also

## 6. WB5: the m5k6 cell closes without OC2 or any side-shape hypothesis

The same exact-kernel mechanism gives a separate theorem. Assume actual Delta10, zero x-frontier, m=5,k=6, and the accepted residual condition that f is the unique low C-minimum and has empty signature. OC1 gives Mx=E disjoint-union {f}. Put Z0={v in U_C(x):v incomparable with f}; it has at most two labels, all full, and E union Z0 is a C ideal. If Z0 is empty the original dual VG already pays, but the following proof also allows that case.

Every Z0 label has a strict E ancestor. Indeed, if some Z0 label had none, choose a minimal label below it in the ideal E union Z0. This minimum would lie in Z0, would be a full C-minimum and hence an x-frontier point, contrary to the hypothesis. Thus no Z0 label can be the first letter of a true gamma in LE(C-f).

Delete f and fix a true gamma. Again its missing-x labels are exactly E, so h=q(gamma)=h_x(gamma)<=5. All legal f gaps form 0..d with d<=7, and d>=h. If h=0, the first gamma label cannot be Z0 and therefore is an actual f-successor containing x. Thus d=0, H=1, and the original root caps give 1<=Y,Z<=6. Section 4a yields the exact identity

    F_beta+G_beta =82+5(Y+Z)-2YZ-2min(Y,Z) >=58.    (WB5a)

For completeness, YZ<=3(Y+Z), Y+Z<=12 and min(Y,Z)<=6 imply the displayed lower bound: the expression is at least 82-(Y+Z)-2min(Y,Z)>=58.

If h>=1, gamma begins in E. Using exactly the same literal f-slots and prefix counts as in Sections 3-4, the kernel F+G has

Summing these true fibers gives

    4K_u+K_y+K_z-2N
       = sum_beta(F_beta+G_beta) >=16e(C)>0.       (WB5)

This is the one fixed physical bank (ub,yb,zb), weights (4,1,1)/6. The same candidate-wise upper bounds give one strictly balanced physical pair. It closes the whole remaining zero-frontier m5k6 cell after the accepted residual reduction, with no OLD moment and no CA dependency.
