# A tempting rank/exclusive-upper strengthening is false

This is a real-poset obstruction, not a new D9 bank and not an NB counterexample.
The proved `RANK_GAP_ANALYTIC.md` bound remains unchanged.

For `D(x) subset D(y)`, write `r` for the guaranteed rank interval used in that theorem and `k=|U(y)\U(x)|`. The tempting replacement

`P(x<y) >= (r+1)/(r+k+2)`

is false, even at incomparability degree 9. Consequently one must not use it to bypass the true deletion-slot denominator. A preliminary source census would have spuriously declared 74 extra original bank occurrences payable.

## Exact control

Take ten labels `x,a,y,b1,b2,b3,c1,c2,c3,c4`, with only the relations generated by

- `a<y<bi` for all three `bi`;
- `a<cj` for all four `cj`;
- `x` is isolated.

The transitive predecessor masks are `(0,0,2,6,6,6,2,2,2,2)`.
All incomparability degrees are at most 9. Here `D(x)` is empty, `r=|D(y)|=1`, and `k=3`, so the proposed bound would be `1/3`.

After deleting `x`, `a` must be first. Among the remaining eight positions, `y` is the first of a uniformly positioned four-label set `{y,b1,b2,b3}`. Its expected rank among those eight positions is `9/5`; its expected full deletion-word rank is `14/5`. The isolated label `x` has ten insertion gaps for every deletion word. Thus, with the exact original uniform-extension law,

`P(x<y) = (14/5)/10 = 7/25 < 1/3`.

The DP in `RANK_EXCLUSIVE_RATIO_CONTROL.py/.json` independently counts `e(P)=100800` and `28224` extensions with `x<y`. The side branches above `a` are what defeat the putative ratio: knowing only the interval count and exclusive upper set does not control when that common interval begins under the original law.

The more conservative, proved rank-gap lower bound gives `2/10=1/5`, consistent with this control. No existing analytic replacement count changes.
