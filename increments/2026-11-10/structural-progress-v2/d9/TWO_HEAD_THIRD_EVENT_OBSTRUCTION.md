2026-11-10. Exact control; internal independent review PASS in TWO_HEAD_THIRD_EVENT_INDEPENDENT_REVIEW.md. This is distinct from the earlier three-common-leaf obstruction: the tail here has TWO minimal heads and one descendant.

Fix Q=(0,0,1,1,7,7,23), physical labels a,b,u,v,p,q,z, and reference abuvpqz. Tail order is p<z with q incomparable to both. Retain anchors A={u<b}, B={v<u}. Therefore no fixed nonnegative coefficient bank on A,B and that single C can reach one third of its coefficient sum uniformly over these completions. This does not forbid a fourth event, changing the anchors, or a condition-dependent bank.
